My map isn't syncing to my other device

trvld syncs through iCloud. Make sure both devices are signed in to the same Apple ID and that iCloud is turned on for trvld in Settings › [your name] › iCloud. Changes usually appear within a minute.

A country is too small to tap

Use the list button in the bottom-right corner to search for any country or state by name.

Why are some places not counted toward 195?

The count covers the 193 UN member states plus the Vatican and Palestine. Territories like Puerto Rico and Greenland, and partially recognized countries like Kosovo and Taiwan, can still be checked off. They're shown under "Other places" in your stats.
