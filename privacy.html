Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

trvld is a travel map for keeping track of the countries, US states and national parks you've visited, and the trips you've taken. It's available for iPhone and Android, and it's built to keep your data yours. trvld is made by Estes Labs LLC, an Indiana company.

What we collect

Nothing. trvld has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking. We don't run any servers that receive your data.

Where your data lives

Everything you add to trvld (the places you've marked as visited, your trips and their dates and notes, and the photos and videos you attach) is stored on your device. Depending on your phone, it's also kept in one of these places, which only you can access:

  • iPhone: your own private iCloud account, if you're signed in to iCloud. This lets your map sync between your devices and come back if you reinstall the app. This data is stored by Apple under your Apple Account, and we can't access it.
  • Android: your Google account's device backup, if Android backup is turned on. Android backs up your places, trips and notes so they come back on a new phone. Photos and videos are too large for this backup; you can save them yourself with Back up to a file in the app, and that file goes wherever you choose to save it. We can't access either one.

Photos and videos

trvld uses the system photo picker, so the app only receives the specific photos and videos you choose. It doesn't get access to the rest of your library. trvld saves a resized copy of each photo and a compressed copy of each video you pick.

Purchases

trvld Pro is a one-time in-app purchase. Payment is handled entirely by the App Store or Google Play. We never see your payment details; the app only learns whether you own trvld Pro.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes the data stored on your device.

  • iPhone: to also remove the iCloud copy, open Settings › [your name] › iCloud › Manage Storage › trvld and delete its data.
  • Android: to manage or delete the Google backup, open Settings › Google › Backup (the exact wording varies by phone). Delete any backup files you saved yourself wherever you stored them.

Children

trvld doesn't knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new date.
